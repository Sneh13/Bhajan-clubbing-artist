What Is Bhajan Clubbing?

Bhajan Clubbing is a modern live devotional music format that brings traditional bhajans, kirtan, rhythm and audience participation together in an energetic setting. It can be adapted for private celebrations, weddings, corporate gatherings, community events and larger devotional programmes.

Unlike a conventional bhajan programme, the focus can be more interactive, with guests singing along, clapping and participating with the live performers. The exact musical format is customised to suit the event, venue and audience.
